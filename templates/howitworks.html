How it works

Four steps between a half-formed opinion and an argument that actually holds up.

1

State your position

Type what you actually believe, in your own words. Any topic works, from casual takes to competition prep.

2

Pick the intensity

Gentle for a first try, firm but fair for regular practice, or relentless if you want a real fight.

3

Go back and forth

It argues the strongest opposite case it can. You defend, it pushes back again. No agreeing, no letting you coast.

4

Get your verdict

End the debate anytime for an honest score: your strongest point, your weakest, and one way to argue it better next time.
